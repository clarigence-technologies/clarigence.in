PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy.
In plain language.

What we collect through this website, why we collect it and the choices you have.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who we are

Clarigence.in is an IT services and digital solutions company based in Nashik, India. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it and the choices you have. If you have a question, email us at hello@clarigence.in.

Information you give us

When you send an enquiry through our website, we collect your name, email address, the service you’re interested in and your message. We also collect your phone number and company name if you choose to provide them.

If you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive whatever you send through that service.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you.
  • To keep this website secure and working properly.
  • To understand how people use this website.

We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use this website. It sets cookies named _ga and _ga_*, which can last for up to two years. It collects information such as the pages you view, how far you scroll, the links you click, your device and browser type, the website that referred you and your approximate location. We also record that an enquiry was sent and which service it was about, but never your name, contact details or message. Google keeps this analytics data for up to 14 months.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Services that handle information for us

  • Hostinger hosts this website and the database that stores enquiries. Our web server keeps standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, browser details and the pages requested.
  • Our email provider delivers enquiry notifications to our team.
  • Google Fonts supplies this website’s fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • Google Analytics receives the information described above.

These providers may process information outside India. We may also share information if the law requires it. Links to LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and WhatsApp take you to services with their own privacy policies.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond to you and for our normal business records. You can ask us to delete yours at any time, unless we’re required by law to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you access to your personal information, correct it or delete it, and you can raise a concern. To do any of these, email hello@clarigence.in. Depending on where you live, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have further rights.

How we protect information

This website uses HTTPS, and only signed-in administrators can view enquiries. No online system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children. If you’re under 18, please don’t send us personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the date at the top of this policy.
